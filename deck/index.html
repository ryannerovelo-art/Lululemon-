The Problem

Culture typecast the brand as athleisure for people who aren't athletes.

The "soccer mom in Aligns who never works out" joke undersells the real customer: disproportionately ambitious, working, and actually training. Her image drifted toward leisure. Her life is anything but.

02 / 09
lululemon · power moves
The Insight

She's not two people managing two identities.

People don't clock out of ambition to work out, and they don't clock out of discipline when they leave the gym. It's the same person, same intensity — different room.

lululemon · power moves
The Big Idea

Dress her for the parts of the day that actually demand something.

We follow the same woman from a 6AM lift straight into a 9AM pitch — same energy, same posture, same standard, no wardrobe-mandated personality change required.
