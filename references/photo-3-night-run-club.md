# Reference 3 — Night Run Club

**Proof point:** The 5:30AM (or 9PM) run club is already full.

## Description

A candid night-time photo of a group run in an urban setting — a lit
track/path along a street, modern buildings in the background, purple/blue
ambient lighting suggesting evening or pre-dawn.

In the foreground, a woman jogs toward the camera wearing:
- A white/cream cap
- A cream/beige sports bra
- Pink running shorts
- White sneakers
- Holding a phone or small device in one hand

Behind her, several other runners and walkers are visible mid-stride,
indicating a group run club rather than a solo workout — men and women,
casual dark athletic wear, clearly a recurring, organized activity rather
than a staged shoot.

## Why it matters to the campaign

This is everyday proof that disciplined, unglamorous training (a run club
that meets in the dark, before or after a workday) is already a real,
widespread behavior. The campaign doesn't need to invent this person — it
needs to stop cutting her out of the advertising.
