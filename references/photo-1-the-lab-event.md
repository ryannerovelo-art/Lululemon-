# Reference 1 — "The Lab" Training Event

**Proof point:** The Lab already casts real strength.

## Description

A lululemon-branded group photo, "THE LAB," from a training/competition event.
Five people pose together in a gym space with kettlebells and training
equipment visible in the background:

- Two men stand in the back, both in grey hoodies (one reads "SWEAT IS TONIC"),
  one with arms relaxed at his sides, the other with arms crossed.
- A woman stands between them in the back row, arms crossed, dark hair.
- Two women kneel/sit in the front row: one wears a black sports bra
  reading "TRAIN LIKE [something]" with dark leggings, seated on a kettlebell;
  the other wears a light blue/grey sports bra and grey sweatpants, also
  seated on a kettlebell.
- The lululemon wordmark appears at the top of the frame.

Caption/fine print at the bottom of the image:
> "Limited spots available. Ticket purchaser must show competitor ticket at
> check-in. Additional tickets may be purchased for guests (non-competitors)."

## Why it matters to the campaign

This is lululemon's own event photography — real, visibly strong, mixed-gender
athletes training as a community, not a solo influencer shoot. It's evidence
the "Power Moves" casting brief doesn't need to be invented; the brand already
has the raw material in its own event marketing.
