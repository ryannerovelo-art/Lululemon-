## Description

(reads as a London/European city street).

She's wearing:

- A ribbed grey/blue sports bra underneath
- Matching grey jogger-style sweatpants
- Black sunglasses
- A layered statement necklace
- A small quilted silver crossbody bag
- Black strappy heeled sandals

A Pinterest visual-search overlay ("Try it on" button with a camera icon) is
visible in the bottom-left corner, indicating this was sourced from a
Pinterest street-style/shopping context.

## Why it matters to the campaign

This is the campaign's central visual thesis already happening in the wild,
unprompted: athletic wear (sports bra, joggers) paired directly with
boardroom-coded pieces (tailored blazer, heels, statement jewelry). It
becomes a literal hero look for the campaign rather than a designed metaphor.
