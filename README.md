# lululemon — Power Moves (Campaign Concept)

A campaign concept repositioning lululemon away from the "athleisure for people
who aren't athletes" stereotype, toward performance wear for people whose day
is the competition — in the gym and in the boardroom.

- [`CAMPAIGN_BRIEF.md`](./CAMPAIGN_BRIEF.md) — full creative brief: insight, audience, big idea, tagline, messaging pillars, executions, sample copy, KPIs.
- [`site/index.html`](./site/index.html) — campaign landing page concept. Open directly in a browser, no build step.
- [`deck/index.html`](./deck/index.html) — presentation deck (9 slides). Open in a browser, navigate with arrow keys or the dots at the bottom.

Both `site/` and `deck/` are self-contained static HTML/CSS/JS — no dependencies, no build step.
